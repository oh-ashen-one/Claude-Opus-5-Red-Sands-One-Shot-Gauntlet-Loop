# The bar: Ghost of Tsushima, judged honestly

You are comparing our frame against **Ghost of Tsushima (Sucker Punch, 2020)**. Load real
reference into your own eyes before judging — search for and view actual GoT screenshots
(pampas fields, Iki sunset, red maple forest, coastal ride, standoff duel). Judge our frame
next to those. Never judge from the builder's description of the frame; only from the PNG.

## What GoT actually looks like — the things to check for

**Light.** One low, warm key. Long shadows with visible direction. Shadowed areas are never
black — they hold cool blue-violet bounce. Highlights bloom softly, never blow out to white
mush. Aerial perspective: every distance band is a distinct value, so depth reads instantly.

**Colour.** Highly saturated but *controlled* — one dominant hue per vista (gold, crimson,
jade, indigo) with a single complementary accent. Never muddy brown-grey everywhere. Never
the flat uniform green of an untuned engine.

**Wind.** Everything moves, and everything moves *together* along one direction. Grass bends
in travelling waves, not per-blade jitter. Leaves and petals stream through the air on the
same vector. Cloth trails behind. Wind is the single most identifiable thing in the game.

**Density and scale.** Fields are dense enough that ground is barely visible, and blades read
at correct human scale — roughly knee-to-waist on the character, never taller than the camera.
Silhouettes are layered: near grass, mid trees, far ridges, distant peaks.

**Composition.** Frames are staged like landscape photography — strong horizon placement,
leading lines, a clear subject. The character reads as a small dark figure against a vast
lit landscape.

**Restraint.** Almost no HUD. No floating icons. No debug text.

## Automatic fail conditions

- Sky is black, banded, or blown-out white at the zenith
- Camera clipped inside geometry, or the player is not visible when they should be
- Anything reads as flat-shaded untextured polygons
- Grass/trees at wrong scale relative to the character
- Frame is uniformly one muddy value with no depth separation
- Visible seams, z-fighting, popping, or hard LOD edges
- FPS below 45 at 1080p
